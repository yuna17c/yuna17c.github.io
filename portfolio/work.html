RESEARCH PUBLICATION PUBLICATION 01

"Hi Alex" or "Dear Dr. Morgan"?: Exploring How AI-suggested Politeness Strategies Influence Email Writing and Social Perception Among Native and Non-native Speakers

Yuna Choi, Zibo Selena Zhang, Ziang Xiao, Q. Vera Liao, Jian Zhao

CSCW · 2026

I participated in this paper as an undergraduate student at the Human-Computer Interaction Lab at the University of Waterloo. The paper explores the impact of AI-generated autocomplete suggestions on writing style, tone, and social perception. I developed a full-stack web application that provides auto-complete suggestions with different politeness levels using OpenAI API. I also built an interaction tracking framework enabling session replay and downstream research analysis.
